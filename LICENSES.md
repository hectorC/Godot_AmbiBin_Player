# Third Party Dependencies

- SAF core and the AmbiBIN example have ISC notices. The Windows build enables the SOFA reader with its bundled BSD-3-Clause libmysofa. Tracker and HADES are disabled. The bundled resources retain their own notices, including MIT for afSTFT, convhull_3d, and md_malloc, BSD-style terms for KissFFT and the Speex resampler, and the zlib license for zlib. Preserve the notices in the pinned source. [SAF license](https://github.com/leomccormack/Spatial_Audio_Framework/blob/18fd5aba46e20787b51f28f7197a68506c965c07/LICENSE.md)
- The embedded HRIR source carries an ISC notice and identifies the dataset as a Genelec Aural ID of a KEMAR dummy head provided by Aki Mäkivirta and Jaan Johansson. Retain that attribution and confirm any separate dataset terms before public binary distribution. [Embedded HRIR source](https://github.com/leomccormack/Spatial_Audio_Framework/blob/18fd5aba46e20787b51f28f7197a68506c965c07/framework/modules/saf_hrir/saf_default_hrirs.c)
- dr_libs offers the Unlicense and MIT No Attribution. The build fetches the upstream source with its license intact. [dr_libs license](https://github.com/mackron/dr_libs/blob/f12e197fd462e4c22873fbd70d11b1d2f2c1dc3c/LICENSE)
- Intel oneMKL is provided by a separate local installation. The renderer statically links oneMKL, so redistribution review must cover the linked Intel components as well as any DLLs packaged later. Use the installed oneMKL 2025.3 terms in `share/doc/mkl/licensing/license.rtf`.
- Godot and godot-cpp use the MIT license. The GDExtension links godot-cpp alongside SAF and its dependencies. [Godot license](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/c51ff06e8836a70ea905701c081bf94124dcd6ce/LICENSE.txt) · [godot-cpp license](https://github.com/godotengine/godot-cpp/blob/2a4c066e1e8c121046e9d35fe91cc8e726861c48/LICENSE.md)

Godot AmbiBin Player is licensed under [GPL-3.0-or-later](LICENSE), matching the SPARTA AmbiBIN source headers. Complete third-party redistribution notices and compatible Windows binary dependencies are required before publishing a combined Asset Library download.
