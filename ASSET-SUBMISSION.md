**Summary.** Play ambiX recordings in Godot and hear the soundfield rotate relative to a Camera3D listener.

**Description.** Godot AmbiBin Player uses SAF AmbiBIN to decode first- through fifth-order ambiX WAVs into binaural stereo for headphones. The player mixes with other sources through Godot's standard audio mixer. It supports playback controls, looping, adjustable decoder settings, and custom SOFA HRIRs imported into the project.
